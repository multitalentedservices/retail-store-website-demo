How reserve-for-pickup works

1

Reserve from the catalogue

Add items to your pickup basket. Quantities can never exceed the stock on the shelf — the site stops you and says why.

2

Pick a pickup time

Choose a time slot and leave your name and mobile number. You get a reservation reference straight away.

3

Pay at the counter

Collect from the store and pay there. Pay at pickup — this demo takes no payment, and no card details are ever asked for.

Regular items, kept ready

Refill & regulars list — demo module

Some items come home again and again — the same vitamins, the same baby wipes, the same hand wash. Register a regular item here and this demo keeps your list in this browser, so the pattern a real counter would follow is visible end to end: register → reminder rhythm → reserve for pickup.

Demo only: the list is stored only in this browser (localStorage), nothing is sent anywhere, and no item is supplied through this website. Prescription medicines are dispensed in-store only against a valid prescription and are not sold through this demo website.
